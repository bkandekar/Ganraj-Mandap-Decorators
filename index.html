About Ganraj Mandap Decorators

Crafting Sacred & Majestic Mandaps with Devotion & Precision

Established in Kalwa (East), Thane, Ganraj Mandap Decorators has been the premier partner for hundreds of prestigious Sarvajanik Ganeshotsav Mandals, residential housing societies, and grand family celebrations for over 12 years.

We bring together master architectural carpenters, skilled fiber sculptors, certified electrical safety engineers, and creative lighting designers. From landmark temple replicas like Ayodhya Ram Mandir to historic Maratha forts and eco-friendly bamboo sanctuaries, we ensure every inch reflects devotion, grandeur, and uncompromised safety.

  • Heavy-Duty Weatherproof Scaffolding: High-tensile iron pipe & German waterproof tarpaulin setup that withstands heavy monsoon winds.
  • Municipal & Fire Compliance: Complete assistance with fire-retardant fabric coatings, emergency exit layouts & electrical load distribution.
  • Respectful Visarjan & Site Cleanup: Seamless stage dismantling and clean handover within 24 hours of Anant Chaturdashi.
