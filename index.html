About Ganraj Mandap Decorators

Crafting Sacred & Majestic Mandaps with Devotion & Precision

Established in Kalwa (East), Thane, Ganraj Mandap Decorators has been the premier partner for hundreds of prestigious Sarvajanik Ganeshotsav Mandals, residential housing societies, and grand family celebrations for over 12 years.

We bring together master architectural carpenters, skilled fiber sculptors, certified electrical safety engineers, and creative lighting designers. From landmark temple replicas like Ayodhya Ram Mandir to historic Maratha forts and eco-friendly bamboo sanctuaries, we ensure every inch reflects devotion, grandeur, and uncompromised safety.

  • Heavy-Duty Weatherproof Scaffolding: High-tensile iron pipe & German waterproof tarpaulin setup that withstands heavy monsoon winds.
  • Municipal & Fire Compliance: Complete assistance with fire-retardant fabric coatings, emergency exit layouts & electrical load distribution.
  • Respectful Visarjan & Site Cleanup: Seamless stage dismantling and clean handover within 24 hours of Anant Chaturdashi.

For residential housing society pandals and traditional mandaps, bookings can be finalized 2 to 3 weeks prior to Ganesh Chaturthi, subject to slot availability.

Yes, absolutely. Safety is our top priority. We use certified ISI-marked flame-retardant canvas/tarpaulins, armored copper electrical wiring with dedicated Earth leakage circuit breakers (ELCB/MCBs), and provide strategically placed ABC dry-powder fire extinguishers. We assist your committee with all structural safety certificates required by local police and municipal authorities.

Yes! Our senior surveyor conducts a thorough on-site visit to measure the exact length, width, overhead electrical cable clearance, and traffic passage requirements. We then customize the 3D proportions of the temple/fort facade to perfectly fit your ground without violating municipal guidelines.

We operate on a transparent, milestone-based structure:
• 25% Advance on booking to lock dates and start material preparation.
• 50% on Structure Erection at the site 2 days before Ganesh Sthapana.
• 25% Balance on the first day of festival after complete lighting and sound testing.
We accept Bank Transfers (NEFT/IMPS), UPI, and Cheque with formal receipts.

Yes. Dismantling is included in our contract. Our team commences polite and structured dismantling immediately after your Mandal's Visarjan and ensures the entire street/compound is swept and handed back in clean condition within 24 hours.
